# Retirement Resilience research agenda

## Near-term outputs

1. A beneficiary-centered problem brief describing adequacy, solvency, access, and transition risks.
2. A comparison of financing and benefit-design options, including impacts by age, income, caregiving status, disability, and work history.
3. A transition-protection model for people near retirement and people already receiving earned benefits.
4. A public fiscal and outcome dashboard with assumptions that can be challenged and revised.

## Example research questions

- Which options improve long-term benefit resilience without pushing investment or longevity risk onto people least able to bear it?
- What protections are needed for people who are near retirement or already receiving benefits?
- Which state, local, employer, or federal levers can be tested without overstating what a pilot can prove?

## Evidence standard

Use transparent fiscal assumptions, peer-reviewed research where available, and disaggregated impact analysis. Do not present an aggregate result as fair without showing who bears the tradeoff.
